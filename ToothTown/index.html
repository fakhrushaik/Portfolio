Privacy statement

ToothTown v2 is a demonstration. All patients, bookings, and staff names are fictional demo data. Nothing here is real patient health information, nothing is sold or shared, and on GitHub Pages your demo changes are stored only in your own browser (localStorage). There are no accounts, payments, or real clinical records, and this app makes no certification claim.

Why I made this website

This project explores how a small dentistry clinic could run its day clearly: one plan, one schedule, and one approval queue, instead of scattered notes. The goal is a calm tool the front desk can actually use — easy to read at a glance, hard to double-book, and clear about what still needs a doctor's decision.
