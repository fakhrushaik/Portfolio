ToothTown

A 3D dentistry clinic app, built from a hand-drawn floor plan.

Open the ToothTown demo → · Back to portfolio

What it is

A SeatSavor-style clinic booking app: rooms from the sketch, staff, patient scheduling, a calendar, and available slots. New bookings stay pending until a doctor approves or rejects them.

What I built

  • Clinic rooms including operatories, X-Ray, reception, doctor offices, waiting area, and storage
  • Staff and demo patients, with appointments saved in the full version
  • Calendar and available-slots view for daily operations
  • Doctor approval workflow for new bookings, and full access for the IT admin role in the full version
  • Accessibility, privacy, and project-background sections

Current status

A professional VS Code rebuild is in progress, with a teal/slate palette and a more operations-focused scheduling UI, replacing the earlier game-like version. The portfolio demo here should be the static version of that rebuild once it is ready.

Privacy

Demo only. Fictional patients, staff, and appointments. No real clinic data, no payments.
