SeatSavor

Pick your table in 3D, like booking a seat on a plane.

Open the SeatSavor demo → · Back to portfolio

What it is

A showcase-first 3D restaurant table-booking app. A pastel isometric room with 12 clickable tables, detail cards for each table, and camera presets for the opening view, top down, window side, and bar corner.

What I built

  • 12 tables (T1–T12) with free, occupied, and selected states
  • Click a table in 3D, or from the list, to open its detail card: seats, shape, dimensions, zone, and location note
  • Smooth orbit camera with gentle zoom, zoom buttons, and a zoom-speed setting
  • Light/dark mode and an accessibility mode: larger text, high contrast, reduced motion, visible focus, and keyboard navigation
  • Node.js + Express demo backend in the full project: tables and in-memory demo bookings only, no payments, no accounts

Tech

JavaScript, Three.js, HTML/CSS, Node.js, Express.

Privacy

Demo only. Fictional data, nothing leaves the page in the static version, no cookies for tracking, no accounts, no payments.
