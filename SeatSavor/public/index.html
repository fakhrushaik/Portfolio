Camera

Camera presets

Poster · Top down · Window · Bar

One tap moves the camera to the poster view, a top-down plan, the window side, or the bar corner. Zoom is gentle, with + and − buttons and a zoom-speed slider.

Accessibility

Built to be usable

Keyboard · Large text · High contrast

Turn on Accessibility mode in the demo for larger text, high contrast, and reduced motion. Tables can be picked with Tab, arrow keys, and Enter, and focus is always visible.

Appearance

Light & Dark

Glass adapts to your theme

The Liquid Glass panels adapt to light and dark mode. Your choice is saved in your own browser only, and the demo starts from your system setting.

Privacy

Privacy first

No tracking · No accounts

No tracking, no advertising cookies, no payments, and no accounts. Theme and zoom choices stay in your own browser. This is a demo, not a real reservation system.

Booking

Demo booking

In the 3D demo

Pick a table, then try the demo booking form in the 3D view. On GitHub Pages there is no backend, so bookings need the local server. See About for the full note.
